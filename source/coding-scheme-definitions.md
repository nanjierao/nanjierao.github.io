# Interest-information coding scheme: definitions only

Adapted from Rao (2026), dissertation Appendix A, Table A-1 (28 codes in four categories).
The dissertation's anchoring quotations come from study participants, so none are reproduced
here. Use only these names and definitions; write new synthetic examples when you need them.
Attribution on the site: "adapted from Rao, 2026, Appendix A".

How a code is applied: one code per **interest information unit (IIU)**, meaning one distinct fact
a person states about their own interest beyond its name. Naming the interest is recorded as a
structural tag and is not counted. One phrase can carry two units (for example, "love" states both
a positive feeling and its strength).

## Affect
- **affect-valence**: the type or direction of feeling about the interest ("love", "really enjoy").
- **affect-intensity**: how strongly that feeling is marked (intensifiers; "love" rather than "like").
- **allegiance**: loyalty to a specific team, brand or crew; identity-adjacent feeling.

## Function
- **function-physical**: a bodily purpose, such as staying active or exercise.
- **function-psychological**: stress relief, decompression, emotional regulation.
- **function-social**: friends, community, belonging.
- **function-creative**: a creative or expressive outlet.
- **function-cognitive**: mental challenge or problem solving as the reason for caring.
- **function-curiosity**: anticipation or discovery as the driver.
- **qualifying-criterion**: the qualifying clause, i.e., what kind specifically.
- **strategic-intent**: a goal-directed purpose, what the person is trying to achieve.

## Knowledge
- **domain-mechanism-knowledge**: knows how the domain works.
- **tactical-knowledge**: strategy, or how to do it well.
- **experiential-knowledge**: first-person sensory knowledge from doing it.
- **internal-differentiation**: names sub-techniques or sub-types.
- **self-assessed-expertise**: an explicit self-rating of skill.
- **quality-standard**: an evaluative bar for one's own output; internalized taste or standards.

## Engagement
- **duration-of-engagement**: how long overall.
- **frequency**: how often, or habitually.
- **time-investment**: how much time per session or piece.
- **institution-level**: an institutional or organized setting, or level.
- **team-vs-solo**: team or solo engagement.
- **biographical-contrast**: the interest set against a personal backstory.
- **core-practice-description**: what the person actually does, in action terms.
- **active-production**: creator rather than consumer; runs, makes or builds something.
- **commercial-activity**: money, selling, commissions, markets.
- **audience-awareness**: how others receive their work.
- **platform-channel**: names the platform or medium where the practice happens.
