## Hands as Narrators (ICIDS 2026)

A full paper on how writers use hand enactment in story writing. I built a remote web platform
that logged every video scrub and keystroke alongside a WebGazer.js gaze stream, for a two-part
study.

## AR smart glasses for everyday learning (IEEE VR 2020)

A Latin-square, within-subjects study with 25 participants compared two ways for the glasses to
show information. See the [AR smart-glasses build](/builds/ar-smart-glasses/).
