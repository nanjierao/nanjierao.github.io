## Sense–Engage–Govern

A design framework for gamified, LLM-assisted crowd-sourced urban digital twins. It has three
tiers and six dimensions, each a spectrum with a named tension, plus a privacy boundary. It draws
on value-sensitive design, contextual integrity, and self-determination theory.

## CS-UDT: a flood prototype for Jacksonville

Residents take part from a zero-install browser client, so they need no app. An LLM pipeline
parses citizen reports and social posts into hazard events, and a convergence rule decides what
counts as confirmed:
