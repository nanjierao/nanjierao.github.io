In two studies, I checked every claim a model made about a student, one unit at a time: is it
**grounded** in the student’s own material, or **inferred**?

<Figure
  title="What models asserted about students, unit by unit"
  note="“Inferred” means the student’s material doesn’t show it, not that it is false: an inferred claim may still be true. The check compares claims with what the student provided; it does not test truth."
>
  <SplitBar rows={GROUNDING} />
</Figure>

Only grounded units count toward Depth and Richness in my
[interest-identification work](/research/interest-identification/).

## The same convention on this site

The legend at the bottom of every page uses the same idea: a solid underline for something shown
in the source, a dotted underline for an inference. The planned Ask and Life & You pages will mark
their claims this way.
