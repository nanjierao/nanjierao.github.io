## Hands as Narrators (ICIDS 2026)

A full paper on how writers use hand enactment in story writing. I built a remote web platform
that logged every video scrub and keystroke alongside a WebGazer.js gaze stream. In a two-part
study with 24 participants, returning to recorded hand enactments took about a quarter of
writing time. The paper describes four strategies and five behaviorally inferred reasons, with
design implications for authoring tools.

## AR smart glasses for everyday learning (IEEE VR 2020)

A Latin-square, within-subjects study with 25 participants. A speech-triggered display produced
better learning and task scores; an object-anchored display produced higher immersion and
usability. See the [AR smart-glasses build](/builds/ar-smart-glasses/).
